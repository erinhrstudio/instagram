Is your compressor distorting your bass? 🔊 #shorts

Description:
Is your compressor distorting your bass? If it's too fast, yes 🔊

A 40 Hz wave lasts 25 milliseconds. If the release is shorter than that, the compressor stops following the level and follows the wave itself, bending it: new harmonics appear that weren't there. Try it in Reaper: release at minimum, then 100 ms, level-matched.

Sources: FabFilter Pro-C manual · Giannoulis, Massberg & Reiss, JAES 2012.
