One note or two? 🎧

Five sounds. Same note every time, built from 10 pitches. From sound 2 on, one of them is pushed out of tune by 1, 2, 4 and 8%. At some point your brain kicks it out and you hear a second note (Moore, Glasberg & Peters, 1986: usually between 1 and 3%).

At which number did you hear two? 👇
