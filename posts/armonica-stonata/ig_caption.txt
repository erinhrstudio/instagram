Audio Illusion #13: One note or two?

Five sounds, numbered 1 to 5. At which number do you start hearing a second note? Comment before you read on.

It's always the same note, built from 10 pitches that fit one pattern. From sound 2 on, just one of them (the 3rd) is pushed out of tune: 1%, 2%, 4%, 8%. At some point your brain stops counting it as part of the note and you hear it as a separate tone. In the 1986 study by Moore, Glasberg and Peters it usually happened between 1% and 3%, and it changed from person to person.

It's one of the ways your ears tell two instruments apart.

Send it to a friend and compare: same number for both of you?
