TITOLO: One note or two? Comment the number where you hear two 🎧 #shorts

Five sounds, same note every time, built from 10 pitches. From sound 2 on, one of them is pushed out of tune: 1%, 2%, 4%, 8%. At some point your brain stops counting it as part of the note and you hear a separate tone.

In the 1986 study by Moore, Glasberg and Peters it usually happened between 1% and 3%, and it changed from person to person. It's one of the ways your ears tell two instruments apart.
