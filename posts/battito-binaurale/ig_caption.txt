Audio Illusion #5: This beat only exists inside your head Headphones only.

Left: a note at 250 Hz. Right: a note at 254 Hz. Neither one pulses: listen to them one at a time and they're steady. Together, on headphones, you hear a beat 4 times per second. Your brain creates it by comparing the two ears.

These are binaural beats (Heinrich Wilhelm Dove, 1839). No promises about sleep, study or focus: it's an illusion, that's all.
