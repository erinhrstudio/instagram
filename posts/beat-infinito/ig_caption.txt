Audio Illusion #1: This beat speeds up. Forever. When does it stop?

It never stops, because it isn't really speeding up: it's many layers of the same beat, each one twice as fast as the other. As they speed up, the fastest fade out and the slowest fade in, so your ear only follows the ones that are accelerating.

It's the Risset rhythm, the rhythmic twin of the endless scale (Jean-Claude Risset).
