Audio Illusion #19: Which shape is Bouba, 1 or 2?

Most people pick the round one. In 2001, 95% of people matched Bouba to the round shape and Kiki to the spiky one. A 2022 study with speakers of 25 languages found the same match well above chance. Psychologist Wolfgang Köhler made it famous in 1929 with the words maluma and takete. Sounds carry shape, before any meaning.
