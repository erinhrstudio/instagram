Audio Illusion #23: does this tone ever stop? 🎧

A is a gliding tone full of silent gaps: you hear it break. B is the same tone, with the gaps filled by bursts of noise. Did the tone keep going under the noise? Comment yes or no 👇

It didn't. Under every burst there was silence. But a loud noise could have hidden the tone, so your brain assumes it kept going and fills in the missing pieces. It's called auditory induction (Warren, Obusek & Ackroff, 1972), and it's how you follow a voice in a noisy room.
