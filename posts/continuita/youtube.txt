TITOLO: Does this tone ever stop? Your brain says no 🎧 #shorts

A is a gliding tone full of silent gaps: you hear it break. B is the same tone with the gaps filled by bursts of noise, and most people hear it keep going.

Under every burst there was silence. A loud noise could have hidden the tone, so your brain assumes it kept going and fills in the missing pieces. It's auditory induction (Warren, Obusek & Ackroff, 1972), the same trick that lets you follow a voice in a noisy room.
