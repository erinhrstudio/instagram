In front of you or behind? 🎧

Headphones on, eyes closed. Three sounds, A, B and C, all in the same spot: the same sound in both ears, only the pitch band changes. Your brain reads the band as a direction. For most people A (4 kHz) is in front, B (1 kHz) behind, C (8 kHz) above (Jens Blauert, 1969).

Where did you hear them? 👇
