Audio Illusion #12: in front of you or behind? 🎧

Headphones on, eyes closed. Three short sounds: A, B and C. Where is each one: in front, behind or above you? Comment before you read on 👇

They're all in the same spot: the same sound in both ears. Only the pitch band changes. Your outer ear colours sound differently from every direction, so when there's no real clue your brain reads the band as a direction. For most people A (4 kHz) is in front, B (1 kHz) behind, C (8 kHz) above.

Jens Blauert found these "directional bands" in 1969.
