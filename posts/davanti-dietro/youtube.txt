TITOLO: Where is this sound: in front, behind or above you? 🎧 #shorts

Headphones on, eyes closed. Three sounds, A, B and C. They're all in the same spot: the same sound in both ears, only the pitch band changes.

Your outer ear colours sound differently from every direction, so your brain reads the band as a direction. For most people A (4 kHz) is in front, B (1 kHz) behind, C (8 kHz) above. Jens Blauert found these "directional bands" in 1969.
