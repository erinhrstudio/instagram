Audio Illusion #18: Which one tastes sweeter, A or B?

In an Oxford study, people ate the same toffee while listening to two soundtracks. With high, soft notes it tasted sweeter. With low, brassy notes it tasted more bitter. Same toffee. Your brain mixes what you hear into what you taste. Researchers call it sonic seasoning (Crisinel and Spence, 2012).
