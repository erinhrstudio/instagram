Didn't hear anything? You're not alone, and your ears are fine. The Zwicker tone is a faint afterimage: many people only catch it with headphones, at a decent volume, in a quiet room. Phone speakers and background noise usually cover it. Try again with headphones (noise cancelling off, no transparency mode) and focus on the first 2-3 seconds of silence. Some people never hear it at all, and that's normal too.
