Audio Illusion #2: Listen to the noise, then the silence. Do you hear a whistle? 🤫

The noise is missing a slice of frequencies, between 2.8 and 4 kHz. When it stops, the video is truly silent, yet many people hear a faint note for a few seconds, close to the lower edge of that gap. It's the Zwicker tone (Eberhard Zwicker, 1964): your hearing fills the gap with a note that doesn't exist.

Not everyone hears it. Best with headphones, medium volume, in the dark.
