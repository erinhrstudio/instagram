I speed up a gallop: at some point it splits in two 🐎

It's always two notes alternating, high and low, in a gallop rhythm. Slowly it's one gallop. As it speeds up, your brain stops following them as one melody and splits them into two rhythms: one above, one below. The notes never change.

It's auditory stream segregation (Leon van Noorden, 1975). Bach used it to make a single violin sound like two voices.
