One cover on Instagram changed her life: Geneviève Racette 🎙️

A folk singer-songwriter from Québec, she sings in French and in English. Dallas Green (City and Colour) saw one of her covers on Instagram and invited her on stage at Osheaga in 2019. With No Water, No Flowers she won Emerging Artist of the Year at the Canadian Folk Music Awards.

Where to start: The Reasons (I'll Be Alright), Same Old Me, Instagram, X2.

Send it to someone who sings covers on Instagram: they could be next 🎸
