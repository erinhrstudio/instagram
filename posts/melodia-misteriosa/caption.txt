You know this song. Can you name it? 🎹

Every note is the right one, but each is played in a random octave. Almost nobody recognizes it, because your brain finds a melody by its ups and downs, and those are gone.

Diana Deutsch tested this in 1972: people barely recognized famous tunes with scrambled octaves. Once they knew the title, they heard it.
