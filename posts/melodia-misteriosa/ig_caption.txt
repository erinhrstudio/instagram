Audio Illusion #11: You know this song. Can you name it? 🎹

Every note is the right one, but each is played in a random octave: higher, lower, middle. The rhythm is the same too. And yet almost nobody recognizes it, because your brain finds a melody by its ups and downs, and those are gone.

Diana Deutsch tested this in 1972: people barely recognized famous tunes with scrambled octaves. Once they knew the title, they heard it. Listen to the first version again after the reveal.

Send it to a friend and don't tell them the title: how long until they get it? 🎧
