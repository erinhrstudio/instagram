Audio Illusion #15: There are 2 notes. You hear 3.

The file has only two pure tones: one stays at 1000 Hz, one rises. If you hear a third note going down, your ear made it. The inner ear isn't perfectly linear, so two tones together create new ones. The strongest is 2 x A minus B, which falls while B rises. These are combination tones, also called Tartini tones.

Turn the volume up a little. Not everyone hears it, and that's part of the fun.
