Audio Illusion #17: This hum can tell when a recording was made.

The power grid is never exactly at 50 Hz (60 Hz in the US): it drifts up and down a tiny bit all day. Recordings made near electrical devices capture that hum. Forensic experts compare its drift with grid logs to find when a recording was made, or to spot where it was cut. It's called ENF analysis, and London's Metropolitan Police has kept logs since 2005.
