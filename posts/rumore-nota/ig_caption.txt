Audio Illusion #6: The same tiny piece of noise, repeated faster, becomes a note.

The sound never changes: it's always the same noise, looped shorter and shorter. Once per second it's a hiss, ten times is a rhythm, a hundred times is a note. Above 20-30 repetitions per second your brain stops counting them and hears a pitch.

It's the repeated noise studied by Guttman and Julesz (1963). For producers: a super short loop of any sound is already a synth. Try it on your sampler.
