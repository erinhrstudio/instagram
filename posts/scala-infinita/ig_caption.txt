Audio Illusion #7: This sound rises for 14 seconds and ends exactly where it started.

It's the Shepard tone (Roger Shepard, 1964; the continuous version is by Jean-Claude Risset): every note is several octaves at once. The top ones fade out, the bottom ones sneak back in, and your brain only follows the climb.

It's the trick behind the endless stairs in Super Mario 64 and, as Christopher Nolan has explained, behind the nonstop tension in the Dunkirk score.

In your tracks: a riser built this way builds tension without ever peaking.

Play it again and find where it starts over. Then send it to someone who swears they have perfect pitch.
