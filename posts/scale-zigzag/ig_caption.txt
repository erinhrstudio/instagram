Audio Illusion #8: Two zig-zag scales. Your brain puts them back in order 🎧

In the video one scale goes up and one goes down at the same time, and every note jumps from one ear to the other. Almost nobody hears it that way: you usually hear the high notes on one side and the low notes on the other, like two melodies nobody played.

It's Diana Deutsch's scale illusion (1975): your brain groups sounds by pitch, not by ear.
