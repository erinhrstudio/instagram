Audio Illusion #11: how high can you still hear? 🎧

Headphones on, start at low volume. Pure tones climb from 8,000 to 19,000 Hz. Comment the last number you can hear 👇

The cells that hear the highest pitches sit at the entrance of the cochlea and wear out first: with age, and faster with loud music. Your phone speaker, cheap earbuds and app compression can cut the top too, so if it stops early, try good headphones before you blame your ears.

Send it to a friend and compare: who hears higher? 🎧
