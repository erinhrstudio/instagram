TITOLO: How high can you still hear? 8,000 to 19,000 Hz 🎧 #shorts

Headphones on, start at low volume. Pure tones climb from 8,000 to 19,000 Hz. Comment the last number you can hear 👇

The cells that hear the highest pitches sit at the entrance of the cochlea and wear out first: with age, and faster with loud music. Phone speakers, cheap earbuds and compression can cut the top too, so try good headphones.
