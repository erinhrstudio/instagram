Audio Illusion #8: Up or down? Write it before reading, for the first and second pair 👇

Each pair is two notes exactly half an octave apart, made only of stacked octaves: they have no exact pitch, so your brain decides whether the second one is above or below. Different people hear opposite directions.

It's the tritone paradox, discovered by Diana Deutsch in 1986. In 1991 she showed that even the language you grew up with matters: Californians and people from southern England tended to hear opposite directions.

Send it to a friend and ask what they hear: you probably won't agree.
