Audio Illusion #16: Which one changed more, A or B?

A goes down by 15 dB, B goes up by 15 dB. Same sound, same amount of change. Most people say B changed more. A sound getting louder may be something coming closer, so your brain treats it as more urgent. John Neuhoff described this bias for rising sounds in Nature in 1998.

Comment A or B before reading this. Then send it to someone and compare.
